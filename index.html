WONDERKICK

Free Kick Arcade

Twenty stadiums. Twenty keepers. Beat the wall, read the wind, bend it into the top corner.

HOW TO PLAY

AIM — press and drag anywhere on the pitch. The sight moves across the goal.
POWER — the meter on the right sweeps while you hold. Release to strike.
SPIN — drag the ball contact point (bottom right) for curl and dip. RESET returns it to the centre.
WIND — the scoreboard arrow shows its direction. It pushes the ball the whole flight.
KEYS — arrows aim, A/D curl, W/S dip, hold SPACE, G toggles the guide.
